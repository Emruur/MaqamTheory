Learn Makam Theory
by Ear and by Eye

A ten-chapter course on Turkish makam music, built around five great makams — Rast, Nihavend, Uşşak, Hüseyni, and Hicaz. Every scale, interval, and melodic sketch on this site has audio you can play, synthesized at the exact microtonal pitches of the Arel–Ezgi–Uzdilek system, plus staff notation with authentic Turkish accidentals.

How to use this course Read the chapters in order — later chapters lean on the vocabulary of earlier ones. Play every audio example, ideally more than once. Makam is an aural tradition: the theory only makes sense once your ear recognizes what the numbers describe.
